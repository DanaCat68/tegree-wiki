Tegree Description & Brief History

Tegree is a Roblox storm chasing game currently in its very early development stage. The game is being developed by a dedicated two-person team consisting of RENG and Vertex23. As of now, the game has only released its first initial testing version.
The project aims to simulate realistic storm dynamics and vehicle intercepts. Even in this early alpha, Tegree features dynamic weather systems, a variety of interceptors and civilian vehicles, and a robust radar system. The community is growing, and members can stay updated or communicate via the official Discord server.
