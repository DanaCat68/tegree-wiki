Vehicles

Sign in to save Edit ⋮
This page lists all the vehicles available in Tegree. Vehicles are categorized by their intended role. The current Alpha 0.01 economy is highly punishing; obtaining a top-tier interceptor requires significant dedication and risk-taking.
Regular Vehicles (Dealership)
Standard civilian vehicles. Essential for navigating the map and earning initial cash, but they will be destroyed instantly by any direct tornado impact.
[ FORD EXPLORER IMAGE ]
Ford Explorer
The starting vehicle. It is a standard, unmodified SUV that offers a relatively comfortable ride on paved roads. It lacks any protective armor or off-road modifications, so you must strictly avoid the storm's path while driving this.
Top Speed (Paved): 95 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 70 MPH
Price: $15,000
Max Wind Threshold: 105 MPH
[ SUBARU FORESTER IMAGE ]
Subaru Forester
A popular civilian choice for storm spotters. The Forester's symmetrical all-wheel drive and lightweight chassis make it surprisingly nimble on unpaved backroads. It is excellent for quick repositioning, but its thin sheet metal offers zero protection against hail or debris.
Top Speed (Paved): 105 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 85 MPH
Price: $35,000
Max Wind Threshold: 110 MPH
[ DOMINATOR FORE IMAGE ]
Dominator Fore
A customized Subaru Forester equipped with a roof-mounted weather station and a light bar. While it looks like an interceptor, it is still a civilian vehicle. It is highly agile and perfect for navigating debris fields, but it cannot survive a direct hit from even an EF1 tornado.
Top Speed (Paved): 110 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 90 MPH
Price: $55,000
Max Wind Threshold: 115 MPH
[ DODGE RAM 3500 IMAGE ]
Dodge Ram 3500
A heavy-duty pickup. The Cummins diesel engine provides massive torque, making it excellent for towing and navigating deep mud. Its heavy weight makes it sluggish on tight turns. It is the expensive bridge between civilian vehicles and real interceptors.
Top Speed (Paved): 95 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 80 MPH
Price: $85,000
Max Wind Threshold: 120 MPH
Chaser Vehicles (Interceptors)
Armored vehicles designed to deploy anchoring spikes and drive directly into the vortex. These are incredibly expensive to purchase and maintain.
[ TORNADO PUNCHER IMAGE ]
Tornado Puncher
The cheapest entry-level interceptor. It is built from a heavily welded steel frame. It's slow and extremely heavy, but it can withstand an EF2 tornado. The deployment system is rudimentary and takes time, making it a tough vehicle to master.
Top Speed (Paved): 75 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 55 MPH
Price: $120,000
Min Wind Threshold (Undeployed): 90 MPH
Max Wind Threshold (Deployed): 140 MPH
[ TORNADO ATTACK IMAGE ]
Tornado Attack (TA-1)
Based on a custom-built Baja racing truck. It features a hydraulic lowering system that deploys in seconds, creating a suction effect to keep the wind out. It is fast and agile for an interceptor, but lacks the armor to survive an EF4+.
Top Speed (Paved): 110 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 75 MPH
Price: $180,000
Min Wind Threshold (Undeployed): 115 MPH
Max Wind Threshold (Deployed): 155 MPH
[ ARMADILLO IMAGE ]
Armadillo
A low-profile armored truck with a wide, flat bottom. It uses massive hydraulic spikes to anchor itself. It has an extremely low center of gravity, making it highly stable, but it accelerates like a brick and its deployment sequence is painfully slow.
Top Speed (Paved): 95 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 80 MPH
Price: $250,000
Min Wind Threshold (Undeployed): 110 MPH
Max Wind Threshold (Deployed): 220 MPH
Deployment Time: ~21 seconds
[ SRV DOMINATOR 1 IMAGE ]
SRV Dominator 1
The classic storm chasing vehicle. Built on a 2007 Chevy Tahoe, it features bulletproof Lexan windows and an external roll cage. It is the cheapest way to get into the Dominator series, but it struggles in unpaved terrain and has a lower wind threshold than its successors.
Top Speed (Paved): 100 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 75 MPH
Price: $300,000
Min Wind Threshold (Undeployed): 120 MPH
Max Wind Threshold (Deployed): 165 MPH
[ TIV 1 IMAGE ]
TIV 1
A massive, heavily armored Ford F-450 designed for IMAX filming. The steel plate and thick polycarbonate windows make it incredibly tough, but its engine is underpowered for its weight. Reaching the tornado in time is your biggest challenge.
Top Speed (Paved): 90 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 65 MPH
Price: $350,000
Min Wind Threshold (Undeployed): 140 MPH
Max Wind Threshold (Deployed): 165 MPH
[ SRV DOMINATOR 2 IMAGE ]
SRV Dominator 2
Built on a GMC Yukon XL, this vehicle was made famous for surviving the 2013 El Reno tornado. It features improved aerodynamics and a stronger frame. It's a versatile interceptor that can handle nearly any situation.
Top Speed (Paved): 100 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 75 MPH
Price: $450,000
Min Wind Threshold (Undeployed): 130 MPH
Max Wind Threshold (Deployed): 185 MPH
[ SRV DOMINATOR 3 IMAGE ]
SRV Dominator 3
The latest and greatest in the Dominator series. Based on a Ford F-350, it features gull-wing doors and a highly advanced airbag suspension system. It is incredibly heavy, giving it unmatched stability during direct vortex penetration.
Top Speed (Paved): 100 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 75 MPH
Price: $600,000
Min Wind Threshold (Undeployed): 140 MPH
Max Wind Threshold (Deployed): 210 MPH
[ TIV 2 IMAGE ]
TIV 2
The ultimate intercept platform. Built on a Dodge Ram 3500 chassis with a third axle for six-wheel drive. It solves all the problems of TIV 1. With a deployed threshold of 230 MPH, it can park directly inside an EF4 tornado and survive.
Top Speed (Paved): 100 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 80 MPH
Price: $800,000
Min Wind Threshold (Undeployed): 140 MPH
Max Wind Threshold (Deployed): 230 MPH
Special Purpose (Radar)
Mobile weather stations designed to gather data from a distance. They are completely unarmored and will be destroyed instantly if a tornado hits them.
[ DOW 3 IMAGE ]
DOW 3 (Doppler on Wheels)
A retired military truck converted into a mobile radar platform. It is slow, top-heavy, and handles poorly off-road. However, it can scan a tornado's wind field from miles away, allowing you to verify intensity before you risk driving in.
Top Speed (Paved): 85 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 65 MPH
Price: $500,000
Min Wind Threshold (Undeployed): 100 MPH
Max Wind Threshold (Deployed): 110 MPH
Ability: Long-range radar deployment.
[ RAXPOL IMAGE ]
RaXPol
The most advanced radar platform in the game. Built on a Ford F-550, its rapid-scan X-band radar can complete a full volume scan in 20 seconds. It costs more than most interceptors and offers zero protection, but mastering it will yield the most accurate storm data.
Top Speed (Paved): 95 MPH
Top Speed (Unpaved): 70 MPH
Price: $650,000
Min Wind Threshold (Undeployed): 95 MPH
Max Wind Threshold (Deployed): 105 MPH
Ability: Rapid 20-second radar scan.
